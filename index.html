Maru is a 29 years old unemployed man who failed the 9th grade civil service exam for the fourth time due to extreme panic. He became prone to panic attacks after the traumatic events of 20 years ago when his father disappeared on the mountain where they used to go together. Maru returns to that mountain on his mother's advice to perform a memorial service for his father that should appease his father's soul and thus help Maru get a job. On the mountain though he finds himself amidst the yellow dust under attack of a monster which turns out to be a nachal. Surviving that monster attack gains Maru the attention of the Special Civil Service of the Ministry of Environment and he ends up being hired as a rookie in the Ganghwa Island team.
